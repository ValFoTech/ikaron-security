Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What services does Ikaron Security provide?
We offer a full range of security services including commercial and residential building security, canine units, video surveillance systems, security training, and security audits.
Do you provide 24/7 security services?
Yes, our security personnel and monitoring services are available 24/7 to ensure continuous protection and rapid response at all times.
Are your security guards trained?
All our guards undergo professional training in surveillance, access control, emergency response, and customer service before deployment.
Do you offer customized security solutions?
Yes, we design tailored security solutions based on the specific needs, risks, and structure of each client’s property or business.
What is canine-assisted security and how does it help?
Canine-assisted security uses trained dogs and handlers to enhance detection, deterrence, and perimeter control, providing an additional layer of protection.
Do you install and monitor CCTV systems?
Yes, we provide video surveillance solutions including installation, real-time monitoring, and system management for improved security coverage.
Can Ikaron Security conduct a risk assessment of my property?
Yes, we offer professional security audits to identify vulnerabilities and recommend effective improvements for better protection.
What types of properties do you secure?
We secure residential buildings, commercial properties, banks, industrial sites, retail stores, and logistics companies.
How quickly can security services be deployed?
Deployment time depends on the project size, but we prioritize rapid response and efficient setup after assessment and agreement.
How can I request a security service or quotation?
You can contact us through our website, phone, or email to request a consultation and receive a tailored quotation.
